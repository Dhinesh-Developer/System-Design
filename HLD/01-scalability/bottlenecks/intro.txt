What is bottleneck?
- A bottleneck is the slowest/ most constrained part of your system 
that limits overall preformance

Ex: 
Users
  ↓
Load Balancer
  ↓
Application Servers
  ↓
Database ← BOTTLENECK

Adding 100 Application servers won't necessarily help if one database can 
only process 1,000 requests/sec.
